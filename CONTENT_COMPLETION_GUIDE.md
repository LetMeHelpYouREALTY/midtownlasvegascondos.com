## 🎯 Content Quality Guidelines

### Every addition should:
- ✅ Provide genuine value to readers
- ✅ Include keywords naturally (Midtown, Arts District, Las Vegas)
- ✅ Answer user questions preemptively
- ✅ Maintain conversational, enthusiastic tone
- ✅ Include specific details (prices, locations, amenities)
- ✅ Connect to other site pages via internal links
- ✅ End with clear next steps for readers

### Avoid:
- ❌ Keyword stuffing
- ❌ Generic filler content
- ❌ Overly salesy language
- ❌ Repetition across pages
- ❌ Technical jargon without explanation
