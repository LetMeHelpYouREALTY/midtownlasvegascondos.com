## 🎯 Content Writing Guidelines

### Tone & Style:
- Professional but approachable
- Enthusiastic about Midtown
- Factual and informative
- Conversational, not salesy

### SEO Optimization:
- Include location keywords naturally (Midtown Las Vegas, Arts District, 18b)
- Use long-tail keywords (condos in Arts District, downtown Las Vegas living)
- Answer user questions directly
- Include semantic keywords (walkable, urban living, art galleries, First Friday)

### Structure:
- Break into digestible paragraphs (3-5 sentences)
- Use transition words
- Maintain logical flow
- Include internal links where appropriate

### Value Proposition:
Every addition should answer:
- Why should someone care?
- What makes this unique?
- How does this benefit the reader?
- What action should they take?
